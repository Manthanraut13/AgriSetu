Fertilizer Management:
Balanced fertilization based on soil testing:
- Apply NPK based on soil test values, not blanket recommendations
- Split N application: 1/3 at sowing, 1/3 at vegetative stage, 1/3 at reproductive stage
- Apply P and K at sowing (not mobile in soil)
- Foliar spray of 2% urea for quick N response

Organic alternatives:
- Farmyard Manure (FYM): 10-15 tonnes/ha, apply 2-3 weeks before sowing
- Vermicompost: 2-5 tonnes/ha
- Biofertilizers: Rhizobium + PSB + Azotobacter (2 kg/ha each)
- Green manure: Dhaincha or Sunhemp, plough in at 50% flowering

Micronutrients:
- Zinc: 10-25 kg ZnSO4/ha (soil application) or 0.5% foliar spray
- Boron: 10 kg Borax/ha for canola, groundnut, and sunflower
- Iron: 0.5% FeSO4 foliar spray for rice and groundnut

Warning: Excess fertilizer damages soil biology and pollutes water. Always follow soil test recommendations.
