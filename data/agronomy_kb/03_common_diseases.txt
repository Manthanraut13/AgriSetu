Common Crop Diseases and Treatments:
1. Late Blight (Tomato/Potato): Apply metalaxyl fungicide. Remove infected plants immediately.
2. Powdery Mildew: Spray sulfur-based fungicide or milk solution (1:10 dilution).
3. Bacterial Leaf Spot: Apply copper-based bactericides. Remove infected plant material.
4. Root Rot: Improve drainage. Apply Trichoderma viride as biocontrol agent.
5. Leaf Rust (Wheat): Use resistant varieties. Apply propiconazole at first symptom.
Prevention is better than cure - maintain good field hygiene and crop rotation.
