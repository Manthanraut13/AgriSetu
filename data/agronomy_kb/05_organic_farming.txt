Organic Farming Practices:
Organic farming avoids synthetic chemicals and emphasizes natural processes.
Key practices:
1. Composting: Farm waste + cow dung + kitchen waste. Decompose for 30-45 days.
2. Vermicomposting: Earthworms convert waste to nutrient-rich castings.
3. Green Manuring: Grow dhaincha/sunhemp, plough in at 50% flowering.
4. Biological Pest Control: Trichoderma for soil diseases, Pseudomonas for bacterial wilt.
5. Mulching: 8-10 cm organic mulch reduces weeds by 60-70% and conserves moisture.
6. Crop Rotation: Mandatory for breaking pest/disease cycles.
7. Neem-based pesticides: Neem oil (5ml/L) effective against most sucking pests.
