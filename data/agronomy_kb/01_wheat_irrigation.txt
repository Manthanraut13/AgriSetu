Irrigation Guidelines for Wheat:
Wheat requires 4-6 irrigations during its growing season. The critical stages for irrigation are:
1. Crown root initiation (20-25 days after sowing) - most critical
2. Tillering stage (40-45 days)
3. Flowering/Anthesis (60-65 days)
4. Grain filling stage (80-85 days)
Water requirement: 450-650 mm total rainfall equivalent.
Avoid waterlogging as wheat is sensitive to excess moisture.
Drip irrigation can save 30-40% water compared to flood irrigation.
