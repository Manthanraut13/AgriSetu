IPM (Integrated Pest Management) for Indian Crops:
IPM combines biological, cultural, physical, and chemical methods.
Hierarchy: Prevention > Monitoring > Biological Control > Chemical (last resort)

1. Cultural Controls:
   - Crop rotation breaks pest cycles
   - Remove crop residue that harbors pests
   - Use resistant/tolerant varieties
   - Adjust planting dates to avoid peak pest periods

2. Biological Controls:
   - Trichogramma cards for stem borer (release 50,000/ha)
   - Trichoderma viride for soil-borne diseases (5 kg/ha)
   - Pseudomonas fluorescens for bacterial diseases (2.5 kg/ha)
   - NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) for Helicoverpa (250 LE/ha)
   - Beauveria bassiana for whitefly and aphids

3. Physical Controls:
   - Yellow sticky traps for whitefly monitoring (12/ha)
   - Pheromone traps for fruit borers (5/ha)
   - Light traps for moths
   - Bird perches (20/ha) for predatory birds

4. Chemical (last resort):
   - Use only recommended pesticides at correct dosage
   - Rotate chemical classes to prevent resistance
   - Observe pre-harvest intervals strictly
