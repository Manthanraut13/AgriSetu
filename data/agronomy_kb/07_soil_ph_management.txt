Soil pH Management:
Optimal pH ranges for major crops:
- Wheat: 6.0-7.5
- Rice: 5.5-7.0
- Maize: 5.8-7.0
- Cotton: 5.8-8.0
- Soybean: 6.0-7.0
- Vegetables: 6.0-7.5

To raise pH (if acidic, below 6.0):
- Apply agricultural lime (CaCO3) at 2-4 tons/hectare
- Use dolomite lime for Mg-deficient soils
- Wood ash is a cheap alternative for small farms

To lower pH (if alkaline, above 8.0):
- Apply elemental sulfur at 200-400 kg/hectare
- Add organic matter (compost, manure)
- Use acidifying fertilizers like ammonium sulfate
