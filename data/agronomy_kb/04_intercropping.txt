Intercropping Practices:
Intercropping improves land use efficiency and provides risk mitigation.
Best combinations:
1. Wheat + Mustard: 8:2 row ratio. Mustard acts as trap crop for aphids.
2. Maize + Cowpea: 4:2 ratio. Cowpea fixes nitrogen benefiting maize.
3. Cotton + Greengram: 4:2 ratio. Greengram fixes N and provides additional income.
4. Sugarcane + Onion/Garlic: Intercrop in first 3 months when canopy is open.
5. Mango + Pulses: During first 3-5 years when mango trees are young.
Benefits: 15-30% more land equivalent ratio, reduced pest pressure, improved soil health.
