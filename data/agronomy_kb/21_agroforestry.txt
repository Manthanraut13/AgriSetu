Agroforestry Practices for Small Farmers:
Agroforestry combines trees with crops/livestock for multiple benefits.
Systems suitable for small farmers:
1. Boundary Planting: Plant trees on field borders (Neem, Subabul, Glyricidia)
2. Alley Cropping: Grow crops between rows of trees (3-5m spacing)
3. Silvopasture: Trees + pasture + livestock (Mango/Leucaena + grass)
4. Home Garden: Multi-layered system with coconut + banana + vegetables

Recommended species:
- Fruit trees: Mango, Guava, Amla, Lemon
- Timber: Teak, Sandalwood, Melia dubia
- Nitrogen-fixing: Gliricidia, Leucaena, Subabul
- Multi-purpose: Neem (pest deterrent + timber + oil)

Benefits:
- Additional income from tree products
- Improved microclimate (wind break, shade)
- Carbon sequestration (5-15 tonnes CO2/ha/year)
- Soil improvement from leaf litter
- Reduced risk from diversified income sources
