Vegetable Growing Tips:
General guidelines for common vegetables:
1. Tomato: Stake or cage plants. Prune suckers. Consistent watering prevents blossom-end rot.
2. Brinjal (Eggplant): Transplant at 25-30 days. Benefits from heavy feeding (NPK).
3. Okra (Ladyfinger): Direct sow after last frost. Harvest at 3-4 inches for best quality.
4. Bottle Gourd: Requires trellising. Heavy feeder - apply compost before planting.
5. Green Chilli: Pinch growing tips for bushier plants. Reduce watering during flowering.
6. Cabbage: Transplant at 4-5 leaf stage. Needs consistent moisture and high N.

All vegetables benefit from:
- Mulching (reduces watering by 40%)
- Drip irrigation (saves 60% water)
- Companion planting with marigold (pest deterrent)
