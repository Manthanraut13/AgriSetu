Water Conservation Techniques for Farming:
1. Drip Irrigation: Delivers water directly to root zone. 40-60% water savings vs flood.
   - Suitable for: Row crops, vegetables, orchards, sugarcane
   - Cost: Rs 40,000-60,000/ha, pays back in 1-2 seasons

2. Sprinkler Irrigation: Simulates rain. 30-40% savings vs flood.
   - Suitable for: Wheat, groundnut, pulses, vegetables on undulating terrain

3. Mulching: 8-10 cm straw/paddy residue mulch reduces evaporation by 50%.
   - Also suppresses weeds and improves soil organic matter

4. Rainwater Harvesting: Farm ponds (10x10x3m) can store 300,000 liters.
   - One farm pond can irrigate 1-2 hectares during dry spells

5. Bed and Furrow System: Raised beds with furrow irrigation saves 25-30% water.
   - Ideal for vegetables, cotton, and sugarcane

6. Deficit Irrigation: Apply water only at critical growth stages.
   - Reduces total water use by 20-30% with minimal yield loss.
