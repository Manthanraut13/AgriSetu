Post-Harvest Management:
Proper post-harvest handling prevents 15-30% losses.
1. Grain Storage:
   - Dry grain to 12-14% moisture before storage
   - Use hermetic storage bags (PICS bags) for small quantities
   - Metal silos for larger quantities
   - Neem leaf layers between grain layers (natural insect repellent)

2. Commodity-Specific:
   - Wheat: Store at 10-12% moisture, temperature below 25C
   - Rice: Store at 12-14% moisture, proper ventilation needed
   - Pulses: Dry to 8-10% moisture, use metal containers
   - Vegetables: Pre-cool within 2 hours of harvest, maintain cold chain

3. Value Addition at Farm Level:
   - Turmeric: Boil, dry, polish for 10x price increase
   - Ginger: Wash, grade, pack for 3-5x price increase
   - Mango: Dry slices or make pickle for off-season sale
