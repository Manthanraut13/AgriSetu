Cover Crops and Soil Health:
Cover crops protect and improve soil between main crop seasons.
Best cover crops:
1. Legumes (Clover, Vetch, Cowpea): Fix 50-200 kg N/ha. Excellent for low-N soils.
2. Grasses (Rye, Oats, Bajra): Add biomass, prevent erosion, suppress weeds.
3. Brassicas (Mustard, Radish): Bio-fumigation, break nematode cycles.
Timing: Sow cover crop immediately after main crop harvest.
Terminate 2-3 weeks before planting next cash crop.
Benefits: Reduces erosion by 90%, improves water infiltration, increases soil organic carbon.
