Sugarcane Cultivation Guide:
- Planting: Setts with 2-3 buds, planted horizontally in furrows
- Spacing: 75-90 cm between rows, 15-20 cm between setts
- Planting season: February-March (spring) or October-November (autumn)
- Water requirement: 1500-2500 mm over 12-18 month crop
- Critical stages: Germination (30 days), Tillering (60-120 days), Grand growth (120-300 days)
- Earthing up at 60 days and 120 days
- Harvest at 12-18 months when Brix > 18
- Ratooning: Can take 2-3 ratoons after plant crop
- Bio-route: Use Trichoderma viride against root rot
