Millet Cultivation - Climate-Smart Crops:
Millets are drought-tolerant, nutritious, and climate-resilient.
Types and growing conditions:
1. Pearl Millet (Bajra): Rainfed, 25-45 day drought tolerance, 300-500mm rainfall
2. Finger Millet (Ragi): Grows in acidic soils, 500-800mm rainfall
3. Little Millet (Kutki): Very drought tolerant, 400-600mm rainfall
4. Kodo Millet: Tolerates poor soils, minimal inputs needed
5. Barnyard Millet: Fastest growing (45-60 days), flood tolerant

Benefits:
- Require 30-40% less water than rice
- Tolerate temperatures up to 45C
- Grow in poor, acidic, or saline soils
- Higher nutrition than rice/wheat (iron, calcium, fiber)
- No need for expensive fertilizers or pesticides

Market potential: Growing demand in health food segment. 2-3x price of regular grains.
Government support: National Food Security Mission on Millets provides subsidies.
