Reduced Tillage and Conservation Agriculture:
Conservation agriculture has three pillars:
1. Minimum soil disturbance (no-till or reduced tillage)
2. Permanent organic soil cover (mulch or cover crops)
3. Crop rotation

Benefits of reduced tillage:
- Reduces fuel costs by 50-70%
- Prevents soil erosion by 90%
- Improves soil structure over 3-5 years
- Increases earthworm population 2-5x
- Sequesters 0.3-0.8 tons C/ha/year

Transition period: Yields may dip 10-20% in first 1-2 years. Recovery by year 3.
Equipment needed: Zero-till seed drill or jab planter for direct seeding.
