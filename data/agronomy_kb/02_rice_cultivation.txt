Rice Cultivation Best Practices:
Rice is a water-intensive crop requiring standing water for most of its growth period.
- Nursery preparation: Soak seeds for 24 hours, raise nursery for 20-25 days
- Transplanting: 2-3 seedlings per hill, spacing 20x15 cm
- Water management: Maintain 5 cm standing water during vegetative stage
- Drain 7-10 days before harvesting
- SRI (System of Rice Intensification) can increase yield with 40% less water
- Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) reduces methane emissions by 30-48%
