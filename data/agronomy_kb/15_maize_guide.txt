Maize (Corn) Cultivation Guide:
- Varieties: Single-cross hybrids for grain, composite varieties for marginal lands
- Season: Kharif (June-July sowing) or Rabi (October-November in irrigated areas)
- Spacing: 60cm x 20cm for rainfed, 60cm x 15cm for irrigated
- Seed rate: 20-25 kg/ha
- Fertilizer: 120:60:60 NPK kg/ha (apply full P and K at sowing, N in 2 splits)
- Critical irrigation stages: Knee-high, tasseling, silking, grain filling
- Common pests: Stem borer (use Trichogramma cards), Fall Armyworm (use NPV + emamectin)
- Harvest when husks turn brown and grain hardens (moisture 20-25%)
- Expected yield: 4-6 tonnes/ha under good management
