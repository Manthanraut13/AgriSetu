Climate-Resilient Agriculture:
Adapting farming to climate change:
1. Drought-Resistant Varieties:
   - Wheat: HD-3226, C-306 (heat tolerant)
   - Rice: Sahbhagi Dhan (drought tolerant), Nagina-22 (submergence tolerant)
   - Maize: HQPM-1, Vivek-9 (early maturing)

2. Crop Diversification:
   - Replace water-intensive crops with millets (requires 300-400mm vs 1200-1500mm for rice)
   - Pulses and oilseeds are more climate-resilient
   - Agroforestry provides multiple income sources

3. Weather-Based Advisories:
   - Sow crops based on monsoon onset, not calendar date
   - Use short-duration varieties when monsoon is delayed
   - Keep drainage channels ready for excess rain events

4. Heat Stress Management:
   - Irrigate during extreme heat events
   - Use mulching to keep soil temperature lower
   - Choose heat-tolerant varieties for late sowing
