NDVI Interpretation for Crop Health:
NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) ranges from -1 to +1.
Values for agriculture:
- < 0.1: Bare soil, water, or dead vegetation (no crop cover)
- 0.1-0.3: Sparse vegetation, stressed crops, early growth stage
- 0.3-0.5: Moderate vegetation, crops in early-mid vegetative stage
- 0.5-0.7: Good vegetation, healthy crops, active growth
- 0.7-0.9: Dense, very healthy vegetation

Action thresholds:
- NDVI < 0.3: Investigate immediately. Possible issues: water stress, nutrient deficiency, pest damage, or poor germination.
- NDVI 0.3-0.5: Monitor closely. Consider irrigation or fertilizer application.
- NDVI > 0.5: Healthy crop. Continue current management practices.

NDVI trends over time are more useful than single values. Declining NDVI during vegetative growth signals problems.
