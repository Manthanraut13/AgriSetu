Legume Benefits in Crop Rotation:
Legumes fix atmospheric nitrogen through Rhizobium bacteria in root nodules.
N fixation rates:
- Chickpea: 40-80 kg N/ha
- Pigeonpea: 60-120 kg N/ha
- Groundnut: 70-150 kg N/ha
- Green gram: 30-80 kg N/ha
- Black gram: 40-70 kg N/ha

After legume crops:
- Reduce N fertilizer by 25-30% for following cereal crop
- Residual N benefit lasts 1-2 seasons
- Root channels improve soil structure and water infiltration
- Break cereal disease cycles (rust, smut, wilt)

Best rotations:
1. Rice-Wheat: Insert green gram in summer to improve soil
2. Cotton-Chickpea: Chickpea fixes N, improves cotton yield
3. Sugarcane-Greengram: Short-duration legume between ratoons
